01 — The problem

Cameras cannot feel the part.

The defects that get past machine vision are the ones a person would catch by touch — a softer cookie, an underfilled capsule, a cushion compact with a hollow chamber, a patty with the wrong fat distribution, a seam stitched through two plies instead of three.

Today these are caught with downstream lab sampling, X-ray, or a line of human inspectors. Each is slow, expensive, or both. Conet Tactile puts a thin layer of synthetic touch directly on the conveyor and scores every part as it passes.

  • Soft, deformable, translucent, or matte-black products
  • Internal voids, missing fill, layered structure defects
  • Bondline pressure and post-cure mechanical signatures
  • Per-part decisions, not statistical batch sampling

02 — Product

A roll, a box,
a five-minute install.

Conet Tactile ships in three pieces. There is no integration project, no scaffolding, no engineering visit. Your maintenance team installs it during a normal change-over window.

  1. 01

    Tactile Mesh

    A flexible piezo-resistive sheet ships on a roll. Cut it to belt width with the included rotary cutter, peel the adhesive backing, and lay it down. Five-millimetre pressure grid, food-grade laminate.

  2. 02

    Tactile Edge

    A small fanless inference appliance reads the mesh, segments parts, and runs anomaly scoring in under fifty milliseconds. Mounts on the line cabinet with a magnetic base or a DIN-rail clip.

  3. 03

    Five known-good parts

    Run five good parts down the belt and tap calibrate in the web console. Tactile Cloud auto-detects line speed, builds the signature, and is live. Optional bad-sample tuning takes minutes.

  4. 04

    Reject on signal

    Anomaly score crosses threshold, the Edge fires a 24 V relay into your reject actuator. Webhooks, OPC-UA, and PROFINET are supported for downstream MES and SCADA.

03 — Tactile Cloud

The five-sample calibration.

Tactile Cloud is the part of Conet Tactile that learns. From five known-good parts, it builds a per-cell tactile signature and a per-part feature distribution. It runs an inspection-grade one-class anomaly model on top — the same algorithmic family used in modern visual anomaly detection, adapted for pressure-time fields.

Models train on commodity GPUs in minutes and deploy back to the Edge the same hour. Customers who have similar products get warm-start models from our fleet, with strict per-tenant isolation on raw data.

04 — Industries

Where touch beats vision.

Conet Tactile is built for product categories where the defect is physical, not visual. We have deliberately stayed out of the well-served vision-friendly verticals.

Bakery & confectionery

Fill, springback, sandwich-layer count, soft-defect detection on matte and floured surfaces.

Pharmaceutical capsules

Per-capsule fill weight surrogate, deformation, void detection at line speed — without the cost of X-ray.

Cosmetics filling

Pump chamber fill, cushion compact integrity, cap-torque proxy via post-fill pressure footprint.

Technical textile

Ply count verification, seam-thickness uniformity, double-feed detection in continuous web processes.

Semiconductor back-end

Die-attach bondline pressure profile after cure, pre-mold uniformity, package warpage screening.

Processed meat & protein

Patty and fillet thickness, fat-distribution, spring-back, and ply-cohesion at packaging speed.
