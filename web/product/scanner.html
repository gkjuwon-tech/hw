01 — The appliance

An inference box that does not look like one.

Tactile Edge is the appliance you mount on the line cabinet. It reads a 16 × 16 piezo-resistive mesh at 200 Hz, runs the on-board one-class anomaly model, and either fires a 24 V relay into your reject actuator or POSTs a verdict to your MES. Anomaly latency stays under fifty milliseconds at line speed.

We ship the appliance pre-flashed and pre-provisioned to your Tactile Cloud workspace. The first time it boots on your line, it shows up in the desktop app over USB-C and the web console over your network. No engineering visit. No SI integration fee.

  • Tactile Edge appliance, IP54 enclosure, DIN-rail / magnetic mount
  • 350 mm × 1 m starter Tactile Mesh roll
  • Rotary cutter, termination clips, install jig
  • USB-C cable, 24 V DC barrel adapter, mount magnets
  • One Tactile Cloud workspace, pre-provisioned to your email

Engineering reference.

The appliance is built around the Conet Scanner board. These are the numbers your controls engineer will ask for first.

Inference One-class anomaly model on device, < 50 ms verdict latency
Sensing matrix 16 × 16 piezo-resistive, 5 mm pitch, FFC-1.0 mm
Scan rate 1 kHz internal, 200 Hz USB CDC uplink
ADC path 12-bit internal + optional 16-bit external (MCP3564)
MCU ESP32-S3-WROOM-1, 16 MB flash, 8 MB PSRAM, native USB
Connectors FFC-1.0 mm row+col, USB-C, 24 V DC barrel, 24 V relay header
Enclosure IP54, DIN-rail clip + magnetic base, 92 × 78 × 28 mm
Power USB-C 5 V or 24 V DC, PoE+ ready
Mesh roll 350 mm × 1 m starter, food-grade laminate, 5 mm pitch

05 — Before you tap

Frequently asked.

Does the desktop app cost extra?
The desktop app is the Tactile Cloud client. Free to install; the cloud subscription is what is metered. Your trial covers the first month with no per-part cap.
What happens if I cancel during the trial?
The subscription is cancelled, no card is charged, the desktop app stays installed and keeps working in calibration-only mode. The appliance is yours either way.
Can I order more than one appliance?
Yes — bump the quantity field on the order form. Each appliance ships with its own pre-flashed identity and is pre-bound to your workspace. Volume tiers kick in above ten units; contract terms above fifty.
What about customs?
Stripe Checkout calculates VAT, customs, and freight from your shipping address. We ship DDP for KR, JP, and EU; DAP elsewhere. If you need a written quote with a PO number, ping us →
